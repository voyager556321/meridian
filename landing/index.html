Built for people who live in Google — and on Linux.

Browser Calendar + Tasks tabs work. They just don’t feel like a desktop. Meridian is the thin native layer in between.

Calendar and Tasks together

One Today view. Complete a task without leaving the same window as your meetings.

Local-first, your machine

SQLite cache, tokens in the system keyring. We are not building a sync SaaS that rents your agenda back to you.

GNOME-native chrome

GTK4 + Libadwaita. Looks like it belongs next to your other apps — because it should.

One-click Google sign-in

Browser OAuth, then you’re done. No Google Cloud Console for end users.

Free MVP now · Pro later

Free: one account, Today, sync, quick-add. Pro (planned): multi-account and the paid unlock.

Honest about the audience

If you only use Outlook or Apple Calendar, this isn’t for you. If Google + GNOME is your day, it is.
